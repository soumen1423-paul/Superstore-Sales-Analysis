# Superstore Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyses the Superstore sales dataset using Python to understand sales, profit, customer behaviour, product performance, regional performance, shipping performance, and the impact of discounts.

The main goal of this project is to identify important business insights and provide recommendations to improve sales and profitability.

## Objectives

- Analyse sales and profit performance
- Identify the most profitable category
- Identify loss-making products
- Analyse customer performance
- Analyse regional and state performance
- Identify the best and worst-performing months
- Analyse shipping performance
- Analyse the relationship between Sales, Profit, Discount, and Quantity
- Provide business recommendations

## Key Business Insights

### 1. Most Profitable Category

Technology generated the highest sales and profit among the categories, making it an important business segment.

### 2. Loss-Making Products

Tables generated the highest losses, while Bookcases also showed poor profitability.

### 3. Regional Performance

West and East were the stronger-performing regions, while Central and South need improvement.

### 4. Best-Performing Months

September, November, and December were among the strongest-performing months.

### 5. Discount Analysis

Higher discounts had a negative relationship with profit while having almost no relationship with sales.

### 6. Customer Analysis

The top 20% of customers generated approximately 47.96% of total sales. Therefore, the traditional 80/20 rule was not observed in this dataset.

## Business Recommendations

### Increase Sales

- Focus on high-performing products and categories.
- Increase marketing efforts during peak months.
- Maintain sufficient inventory during high-demand periods.

### Improve Profit

- Focus on profitable products and categories.
- Carefully manage discount levels.
- Review pricing strategies for low-profit products.

### Reduce Losses

- Review pricing and discount strategies for Tables and Bookcases.
- Analyse shipping costs, discounts, and product costs.
- Improve inventory management for low-profit products.

### Improve Customer Retention

- Reward top customers with loyalty programs.
- Provide personalised offers.
- Encourage repeat purchases through targeted promotions.

## Conclusion

This project analysed the Superstore dataset to understand sales, profit, customer behaviour, product performance, and regional performance. The analysis identified the strongest and weakest areas of the business and provided recommendations to improve sales, profitability, reduce losses, and strengthen customer retention.
